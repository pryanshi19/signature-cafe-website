The Experience

A rooftop escape above the ordinary.

Signature Cafe is perched above Wongdhen House in the heart of Majnu-Ka-Tilla — Delhi's most culturally layered neighbourhood. With sweeping views of the Yamuna River and the iconic Signature Bridge, every visit feels cinematic.

Whether you come for morning coffee, a slow lunch, or an evening with live music under open skies, this is Delhi dining at its most unhurried.
